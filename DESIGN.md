# ATELIER / DATA
An editorial field guide to Saipranay's work. His transformation of fragmented data into decisions becomes an original source-to-model line drawing, followed by a midnight interactive architecture map.

## Tokens and composition
Porcelain #F5F3ED; ink #171E23; midnight #101820; terracotta #A64E30 (accessible text); copper #C56A45 (decorative); mineral #7CCBB6 on midnight. Fraunces display serif and Manrope sans, self-hosted via next/font. Equal outer gutters, asymmetric 7/5 hero; 1280px content measure. Fine architectural rules support real relationships, never decorative card stacks. Square-cornered controls, restrained pill only for mode switch. Desktop graph becomes a vertical sequence on mobile.

## Motion
One hero line-reveal, opacity/transform 220ms ease-out feedback. No perpetual movement, scroll hijacking, hover-only content, or animation-dependent understanding. Reduced motion disables nonessential transitions. Keyboard focus is immediate.

## Structure
Hero → six-family graph + contextual inspector → three linked editorial studies → source-to-decision sequence → career and credentials → contact. Real fact/dimension example is explicitly separate from the career graph metaphor. Server-rendered prose, small client islands, deterministic typed data.

## Rejected directions
A full-screen dashboard buries identity and overstates live operational scope. A photographic agency template depends on a missing portrait and cannot express architectural thinking.

## Skill application
Project-local skill sources: Emil design engineering, Impeccable 4.7, Taste v2. User's explicit art direction and autonomy override skill requests for style interviews and randomized replacement worlds. Use their craft, motion, typography and browser-review guidance within the supplied world. No photo generation needed.
