# Saipranay Thandu — Beyond the Data

A locally runnable personal-brand website for hiring managers, data leaders and engineering peers. Visitors should understand Saipranay's Engineering / Analytics / Business positioning, inspect architectural judgment, explore three case studies and connect through LinkedIn or email. Beyond the Data is the creative identity; The Living Model is the signature technical explorer.

## Implemented V4 experience

The selected expressive portrait hero (B) introduces the person and value proposition with Explore work and Explore model actions. Both rendered alternatives remain available at `/explorations/hero-a` and `/explorations/hero-b`; the selected direction follows rendered comparison. The authentic portrait original remains unchanged.

A deterministic noise-to-knowledge sequence connects disconnected fragments into an ordered system and settles at three seconds, with Skip, Replay and a completed reduced-motion state. Its isolated route is `/explorations/noise`.

The Living Model retains six families: Data Engineering, Semantic Modeling, Business Intelligence, Decision Systems, Selected Work and Data Architecture. Relationship highlighting, contextual inspection, URL state and keyboard controls support exploration. A separate synthetic dimensional-model exhibit preserves explicit grain, keys, one-to-many relationships, filtering, measures and conceptual DAX. The personal-career graph is not represented as a literal schema.

A native horizontal gallery presents Geospatial Data Pipeline Engineering, Project Delivery Intelligence and Workforce Qualification Analytics with original conceptual SVG artwork, touch/keyboard controls and deeper case-study pages. It never auto-advances. Impact, career, credentials and contact complete the experience.

## Durable constraints

`CONTENT_SOURCE.md` is the factual authority. Preserve typed source content and exact credential verification links. Label synthetic demonstrations and self-reported outcomes explicitly; 180+ integrated layers must not become 180 independently orchestrated pipelines. Do not imply ownership of a statewide program's entire value. Pending award, resume and GitHub remain unpublished until supported. No invented employers, metrics, credentials or endorsements.

The user delegated design decisions and autonomous implementation. The explicit brief supplies product truth; no additional product interview is required. Respect keyboard users, reduced-motion preferences and responsive alternatives to dense desktop diagrams. Local Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind and Motion for React retain the existing architecture and working routes. No accounts, analytics, backend, payments, hosting or deployment are introduced.

`DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` describe the implemented design system. Validation evidence and limitations belong in the separate QA report; this document does not assert unperformed tests.
